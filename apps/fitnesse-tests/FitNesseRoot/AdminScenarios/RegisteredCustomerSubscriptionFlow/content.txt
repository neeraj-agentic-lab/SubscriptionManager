!1 Registered Customer Subscription Flow (P1)

This test validates the complete subscription flow for registered customers.
Tests subscription creation using customerId, followed by modifications, pause, resume, and cancellation.

!|script|RestApiFixture|
|$ts=|timestamp|
|post to|/v1/auth/login|with body|{"email":"admin@subscriptionengine.com","password":"ChangeMe123!"}|

|show|response body|
|$superToken=|json value|token|

!|script|RestApiFixture|

|show|response body|
|$tenantId=|json value|id|
